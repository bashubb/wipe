FAQ

Questions before you clean

Does Wipe upload my photos?
No. Wipe reads and cleans your photos and videos on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded, there is no account and there are no ads.
Will my photos lose quality?
JPEG and HEIC photos stay pixel identical and most videos keep their original video and audio streams. If a file can’t be copied as it is, Wipe tells you and asks before creating a new encoded version.
Are my original photos changed?
No. Wipe creates clean copies. Your originals stay exactly as they were in your library.
Where do the clean copies go?
Save them to Photos, where they land in a dedicated Wipe album, save them to Files, or share them straight from Wipe.
Does it work with videos and Live Photos?
Yes. You can clean photos, videos and Live Photos together in one batch, and Live Photos stay Live Photos.
What does Safe to Share keep?
Camera settings such as exposure, aperture and ISO. It removes location, capture time, device, author, captions and anything else that could identify you or where you were.
How much does Wipe cost?
Your first three cleans are free. After that, one purchase unlocks Wipe for good. There is no subscription, and you can restore your purchase from Settings.
Which iPhones does Wipe support?
Any iPhone running iOS 18 or later.
