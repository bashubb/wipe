Wipe Support

Answers to common questions about removing metadata from photos, videos and Live Photos with Wipe.

Cleaning photos and videos

Does Wipe upload my photos or videos?

No. Wipe reads and cleans everything on your iPhone. Your media, filenames and metadata are never uploaded. See the Privacy Policy.

What do the cleaning presets remove?

Safe to Share removes location, author and copyright, capture dates, device and lens details, editing software and captions, and keeps camera settings such as exposure and ISO. Location Only removes just the location. Wipe Everything removes all metadata that isn’t needed to display the photo or video correctly.

Does Wipe reduce photo or video quality?

For JPEG, HEIC and most videos Wipe copies the original image or video data unchanged and rewrites only the metadata — that’s what Quality preserved means. If a file needs a new encoded copy, Wipe tells you before processing.

Are my original photos changed?

No. Wipe always creates new clean copies; your originals stay exactly as they were. Delete them yourself in Photos if you no longer need them.

Where are the clean copies saved?

In your Photos library, in an album called Wipe. You can also share them directly from the result screen.

Can I clean photos from the Share Sheet?

Yes. In Photos, select items, tap Share and choose Wipe. If Wipe isn’t listed, scroll the app row, tap More and enable it.

Do Live Photos stay Live Photos?

Yes. Wipe cleans both the photo and the video part and keeps them paired, so the copy saved to Photos still plays as a Live Photo.

Purchases

What does the free version include?

A few free cleans so you can try everything. The one-time lifetime unlock removes the limit in the app and in the Share Extension. There is no subscription.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Wipe, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchase while signed in with the Apple Account you used to buy the unlock.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
